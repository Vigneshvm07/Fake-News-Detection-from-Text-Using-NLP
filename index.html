How fake news detection works

NLP techniques can identify linguistic patterns commonly associated with unreliable content.

The system cleans and tokenizes the submitted text before performing analysis.

⚠

Clickbait Detection

Detects exaggerated phrases, sensational language, excessive punctuation and urgency.

◈

Examines wording, emotional language, certainty and suspicious claims.

✓

Combines detected signals to estimate whether content appears reliable, uncertain or suspicious.
